FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it enough on its own?

For most skin, yes. If yours runs dry in winter, add a drop of facial oil on top at night.

Can I use it with Aurum 01?

Yes. Apply the vitamin C serum first, wait a minute, then press in Aqua 02.

Why a jar and not a pump?

The gel is thick enough to stay put in a jar, and heavy glass can be refilled for years. Use clean, dry fingers or the spatula in the box.

Is it suitable for oily skin?

It's oil-free and sinks in without shine, so it works well on oily and combination skin.

How long does a jar last?

About three months used morning and night. Once opened, use it within twelve months.
