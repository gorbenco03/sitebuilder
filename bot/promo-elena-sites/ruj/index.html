01 — The case

A small weight in your hand.

Solid brass, polished by hand and lacquered in the colour it holds. The cap sits on a magnet: pull, and it lets go with a soft click.

38 gof brass, made to be refilled for years

02 — The turn

One turn, all the colour.

Turn the base and the bullet rises, cut at forty-five degrees so the first stroke lands clean along the lip line.

45°bevel for a precise edge

03 — The colour

Satin, not shine.

Eighteen per cent pigment in camellia oil and shea butter. It glides on like a balm, then sets to a soft satin that holds without drying.

8 hof wear from one coat

04 — The shades

Four reds for four lights.

Each colour has its own lacquered case. Pick one and the whole room turns with it.

05 — The refill

Keep the case. Refill the colour.

When the bullet runs low it slides out, and a new one clicks into the same brass. A refill costs €28 and uses 70% less material.
