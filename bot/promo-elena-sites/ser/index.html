FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why is the glass gold?

Vitamin C breaks down in UV light. The gold tint filters UV while still letting you see how much serum is left.

Can I use it with retinol?

Yes, at different times of day: Aurum 01 in the morning, retinol at night. Using both at once can irritate sensitive skin.

My serum turned darker. Is it still good?

Pure vitamin C slowly turns amber as it oxidises. A light golden shift is normal. If it turns deep orange or brown, it's time for a refill.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It's fragrance-free and pH-balanced at 3.2, but 15% vitamin C can tingle at first. Start every other morning and patch test before full use.

How long does a bottle last?

Around 150 mornings at four drops a day. Once opened, use it within six months.
