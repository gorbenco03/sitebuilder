What we collect to run the product

Hidook Site Builder needs a small set of data to let you sign in, edit a site, start a trial, and publish:

  • Account contact (for example email) when you request a sign-in link.
  • Site content you enter (business name, text, photos you upload, design choices).
  • Payment-related status from the card processor when you start a trial or pay (we do not store full card numbers in the builder UI).
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

How data is used

Data is used to operate your account, host your published site, process trial and billing events, and improve reliability. We do not sell your site content as a data product. Third-party processors (email, payments, hosting) receive only what they need to perform their part of the service.

Your choices

You can update site content in the builder, sign out, and cancel a trial before it converts when the product allows. For deletion or access requests once counsel finalizes this policy, use the contact path published for Hidook Site Builder.
