Why cookies (and similar storage) appear

Hidook Site Builder uses browser storage so you can stay signed in, return to your projects, and complete trial checkout without losing progress. Some storage is essential to the product; analytics or marketing cookies are not required to open the builder and edit a site.

Typical categories (placeholder)

  • Essential — sign-in session, CSRF/security, and preferences needed for the editor and publish flow.
  • Functional — remembering UI choices such as last screen when you return to /app/.
  • Optional — any measurement tools the studio adds later will be listed here once counsel finalizes this notice.

Your controls

You can clear site data in your browser settings, use private browsing, or sign out from the builder header. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in or publish. When a full cookie banner is required by counsel, it will appear on the same origin.
