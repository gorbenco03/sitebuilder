# Hidook Site Builder — product contract (local/staging build)

Authority: `VISION.md` is the synchronized source of truth. This file is a compact product contract for workers; if it conflicts with `VISION.md`, `VISION.md` wins.

## Product

Simple website builder sold worldwide. Public name: **Hidook Site Builder**.

- Builder: `https://lp.hidook.agency`
- Customer sites: `https://{slug}.sites.hidook.agency`
- Custom domains: self-serve. The owner enters their domain, the product shows the exact DNS records to create at their registrar, polls propagation honestly (not visible yet / visible but wrong / verified), provisions TLS, and moves canonical, og:url, robots.txt and sitemap.xml onto the new domain. Disconnecting leaves the site reachable on its Hidook subdomain. Apex domains are pointed at www with a registrar-side forward, because the alternative needs account-specific fallback IPs.

## Commercial model

- Stripe subscription with **14-day trial**.
- Card required at signup; no card means no live site.
- Site is live/public immediately after a valid card starts the trial.
- First charge happens automatically on day 14 if the customer does not cancel.
- Price: **99** in the customer currency bucket — EUR (EU), GBP (UK), USD (rest of world).
- Renewal: **29/year** in the same currency.
- Reactivating a canceled site (owner decision 2026-10-05): pay **29** (the renewal price), **no new trial**, not 99 — also for a site canceled during its trial. After payment the site is republished from its latest version immediately and `paidUntil = max(current paidUntil, now) + 1 year` for a site that was already charged, or `now + 1 year` for one canceled in its trial (never charged). The dashboard card states the price and resulting date before the click; the post-payment message is a reactivation message, never "Trial început".
- No promise of permanent hosting from one payment.
- **Event sales via `/elena` (owner request 2026-10-29, one beauty event):** Elena adds a buyer's email on `/elena` (phone page, access code = `ELENA_KEY` env var; without it the page and its API are 404). The buyer gets a sign-in email; the **first** site of that account publishes with no card and no trial, paid for one year (`paidUntil = now + 1 year`, no Stripe subscription — renewal after a year goes through the normal 29/year flow). Elena sees each buyer's site state (not started / in progress / published + address) and ticks „Achitat” when paid in person (100 EUR, cash, outside Stripe). Removing `ELENA_KEY` ends the offer; published sites stay paid.
- Owner creates production Stripe Product/Prices, Customer Portal, refund/cancellation policy. Local/staging uses test-mode/env-driven IDs only.

## Design and templates

New sites are built in the **block editor** (owner decision 2026-10-28; `builder/blocks/`, served at `/app/blocks/`, `templateId: "blocks"`). The catalog offers 13 designs — "De la zero" plus 12 business types, each with its own pages, copy, photos and theme. Customers edit text in place, add/move/duplicate/delete blocks, manage several pages with a shared header and footer, change theme, preview desktop/phone, and publish through the same magic link → address → Stripe trial → live path. Every page is its own HTML file on the published site, with the legal pages, cookie banner and hidook attribution; contact forms deliver to "Mesaje". The Instagram block connects through Instafidget (same `social-feed/*` routes and terms); the public site shows only a connected feed, otherwise the block is omitted. A new design asks once (optional) for business name, phone and town and swaps them in everywhere; the publish dialog lists remaining starter content without blocking, and starter e-mails are never published. See `VISION.md` §3.

Form-editor template scope (kept only for sites already created with it — all test sites):

1. Product/menu businesses
2. Local service / lead-gen
3. Portfolio / beauty / events
4. Professional services
5. Desserdirina remake — root rejected bakery/DESSERD sample promoted into a real approved template, not abandoned sample bytes.

All visible customer/site copy should be Romanian unless a deliberate i18n choice is made later. Every generated template must carry non-editable attribution: `Build by hidook.tech powered by hidook.agency`.

`portfolio` and `professionals` (audit27 S-2B, 2026-09-27) ship an optional "Recenzii" testimonials section, and `professionals` also an optional "Echipa" section — both list-editable in the builder, hidden when empty. This is a small fixed per-template library, not the generic section add/remove/reorder still tracked as backlog in `VISION.md` §4.6. Both templates also gained a general "Scrie-ne" contact form (`portfolio` had no contact form before; `professionals`' existing appointment-request form is unrelated and untouched); it POSTs to `/api/site-messages`, which does not exist yet — until that endpoint and the publish-time attribute fill land, the form always shows its honest tel:/wa.me fallback instead of delivering the message.

## Required product capabilities

- First template load works without refresh on realistic network.
- New sites are made in the block editor (`/app/blocks/`); the old form editor was removed from the builder on 2026-10-28. The three items below describe that retired editor and are kept for history only.
- (Retired) Details drawer opens automatically for every newly selected design; a manual close survives reload for the current design but does not suppress the next design selection.
- (Retired) A first-time visitor starting a template sees a short, skippable 2-step onboarding wizard first (business type → optional name/phone/WhatsApp/town), then the editor opens on the recommended template already filled via the existing `applyQuickstart()`; it never shows again after completion or skip (`localStorage`), and does not change Details-drawer auto-open or the quickstart bar for any later design selection (`PLAN-UX-2026-09-27` §5.1) — except that for the one design the wizard was actually completed (not skipped) on, Details does not auto-open and a small dismissible hint offers it instead.
- (Retired) Identity stamping never invents contact data (VERIFICARE-2026-10-04 H-01): the template's demo Instagram/Facebook links, handle and e-mail (also inside the structured data) are blanked when the wizard/quickstart stamps a name, phone or town, and are stripped from every publish payload while they still equal the template's preset, whether or not the name was ever edited, instead of deriving `instagram.com/<name>` / `contact@<name>.ro`; the publish checklist lists demo social links, e-mail and address while they remain. The wizard/quickstart phone is validated (Romanian local or `+`/`00` international; inline Romanian error) and normalized for `wa.me`; the town also updates the hero label, address town part, the address Google Maps link and the "Unde ne găsești" address. Only the town follows: the street and postal code of the sample address stay the template's (a hybrid address), which the publish checklist flags as "Adresă de exemplu" until the owner types the real one.
- Text, images, hero/section backgrounds and theme colors are actually editable and reflected in preview/live.
- WhatsApp badge is recognizable and supports a user-defined prefilled message. Opening the control shows a horizontally and vertically centered QR in finished customer chrome; the deep-link / QR mechanism is unchanged.
- Social preview artwork is derived automatically from the current hero/business photography; customers are never asked for an `og:image` URL.
- Preview is clear before card/trial.
- Privacy, Cookies and Terms machinery exists; final legal content is owner-gated.
- Export/self-deploy ("Descarcă ZIP" on a live paid site's card in Site-urile mele) uses the live-publish entitlement allowlist: Stripe `active`, `trialing`, or an unexpired legacy paid entitlement. Unpaid, `past_due`, canceled, and expired-`paidUntil` drafts receive a clear Romanian upsell and no file.
- Instafidget is included free for 12 months with Site Builder, then Instafidget Free with watermark; the editor opens in a new tab in the same browser, and disconnected feed is hidden.
- Professional sites can opt in to a native Hidook booking calendar (`appointment.nativeBooking`, per-site flag, built in `bot/calendar-native/`): isolated availability/services/bookings, a public booking widget, an owner dashboard, and email confirmations (local/test harness only, no production sender). Default remains unchanged for sites that do not opt in: the local appointment-request form. (The optional Cal.com link customers used to paste in Detalii was removed from the product on 2026-09-12, owner decision — see `VISION.md` §8/Flow 4; an older site saved with that value keeps it as an unused, harmless leftover in its config.) Opt-out restores the local form without deleting existing native bookings. Cutover to native booking on the existing live fleet is not automatic — see `VISION.md` §8. The switch that turned it on lived only in the form editor, retired on 2026-10-28; sites already opted in keep it.
- **Online booking on block-editor sites (2026-10-28):** the block editor has a **"Programări online"** block (category Contact). In its panel the owner sets the services (name + duration from a list, 15 min to 4 h) and, for each day of the week, whether it takes bookings and from/to which hour (half-hour steps), plus an optional phone for people who prefer to call. The salon, cosmetics, professional services and consulting starter designs include it, with their own services and hours; any other site adds it from the block library. Publishing makes the native calendar match the block: services by name (one removed from the block is deactivated, its past bookings kept), the weekly hours of the default staff member, and — on the first publish only — timezone Europe/Bucharest, 24 h cancellation window, 30-minute start times (15 if a service is shorter than 30 min). The published page mounts the native widget in the site's own colours and fonts; a downloaded ZIP lists the services, the hours and the phone instead, and never calls a Hidook server. Before publishing, starter services/hours left unchanged are flagged with the other starter content. Only the first booking block of a site mounts the calendar; any other shows the static list (the editor says so). Publishing is refused with a clear message when the server has no public address the widget could call from a static host, rather than shipping a calendar that never loads; the calendar is updated after image moderation, all at once. The owner reaches the bookings from the **"Programări"** button on the site's card in Site-urile mele (live sites whose calendar a publish created — it stays even if the block is later removed, so existing bookings remain reachable); there, for block sites, the weekly hours are shown read-only with a link back to the editor and the Servicii tab is hidden — days off, special hours, staff, settings and the bookings themselves stay in that dashboard. The widget opens on the first day of the month that still has a free time, keeps the confirmation in view after booking, and its manage link points at the booking server, not the site's own host.
- GDPR self-service (owner decision 2026-09-27, R-27): the signed-in account's menu in the header of `#dashboard`/`#templates` has "Descarcă datele mele" (JSON export of the account's own profile, sites/version history, orders/invoices, native-calendar services/bookings) and "Șterge contul" (typed-email-confirmation modal, then: cancels any active Stripe subscription, unpublishes and removes every owned site's live files, erases native-calendar tenant data, deletes sessions/login tokens/versions/sites, deletes the user row, signs out). Both routes require a valid session, are rate-limited, and check request origin. See `VISION.md` §5.
- Site contact-form messages (S-2C, PLAN-UX §5.2 supporting piece): a published site's optional generic contact form (added per-template separately) posts to a public, honeypot- and rate-limited API; each owner's dashboard site card shows a "Mesaje" entry with an unread count, opening a modal to read, mark-as-read and delete — the owner is also emailed in Romanian. Messages are included in the GDPR export and erased with their site (or the whole account). A static ZIP/HTML export never carries a live API base, so its form falls back to WhatsApp/mailto instead of depending on Hidook.

## Launch gates — owner only

No push, production deploy, live DNS, real charges, production Stripe, production email sender, or final legal/VAT text without explicit owner approval.

## Done before client delivery

A stranger can use the browser builder end-to-end on the real browser surface: choose template → edit → preview → card test → trial live immediately → edit/republish → export HTML/ZIP → cancel. Export uses the same explicit active/trialing or unexpired-paid entitlement as live publish; unpaid, `past_due`, canceled, and expired sites cannot download files. QA and devil's advocate must open it themselves and save evidence. Client does not QA.
